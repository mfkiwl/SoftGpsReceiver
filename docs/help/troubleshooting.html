Troubleshooting

SymptomCheck
UHD driver not foundInstall UHD, or set the uhd.dll path in Settings > Live.
No devices foundCable, the PC port's static address, firewall, and uhd_find_devices.
Radio found, no satellitesAntenna power, sky view, RX2 selected, daughterboard covers L1, gain.
Signal level barely changes when the antenna is unpluggedThe antenna is probably unpowered, or the bias tee ports are swapped.
OverflowsLower the sample rate, add recv_buff_size=50000000, use a dedicated network port.
Low C/N0 on every satelliteMove the antenna outside, shorten the cable, reduce gain if clipping, look for nearby interference.
Satellites but no positionWait up to a minute, and keep at least four tracked.
No GalileoSample rate must be at least 4 MS/s, with GPS + Galileo selected.
