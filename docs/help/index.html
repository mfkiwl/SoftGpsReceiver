Live receiver setup

The live receiver processes samples from a software defined radio (SDR) as they arrive. A working front-end has four parts:

Active GNSS antenna --coax--> Bias tee --coax--> SDR --Ethernet/USB--> PC
                                 ^
                            DC supply (if the SDR has no antenna power)
