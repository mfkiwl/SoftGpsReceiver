Setting the gain

Watch Signal level on the Live tab. It warns "gain too high" above -12 dBFS or with clipping over 0.5 %, and "gain low" below -65 dBFS. Aim between those, then choose the setting that gives the best C/N0.

Tick Record the samples to disk to keep a session for later (about 20 MB/s at 5 MS/s).
